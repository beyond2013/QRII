Symbol guide
UUniversal set: every element under discussion (the rectangle)
A ∪ BUnion: in A or B (or both). Type | or +
A ∩ BIntersection: in A and B. Type & or *
A − BDifference: in A but not B. Type -
A′Complement: in U but not A. Type ' after the set, or ! before it
A Δ BSymmetric difference: in exactly one of A, B. Type ^
