# Quantitative Reasoning II (BS Artificial Intelligence - 2nd Semester, 2025-2029)

Welcome to the repository for **Quantitative Reasoning II**. This course builds directly upon Quantitative Reasoning I, equipping undergraduate students in Artificial Intelligence with advanced logical, mathematical, and statistical modeling techniques necessary for data analysis and algorithmic problem-solving.

---

## Course Details
- **Course Title:** Quantitative Reasoning II
- **Credit Hours:** 3
- **Program:** BS Artificial Intelligence (2nd Semester)
- **Session:** 2025–2029
- **Pre-Requisite:** Quantitative Reasoning I

---

## Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs)
By the end of this course, students shall have:
1. **Understanding of logic and logical reasoning:** Grasp fundamental and advanced logical principles essential for algorithmic thinking.
2. **Understanding of basic quantitative modeling and analyses:** Formulate and solve real-world problems using deterministic and probabilistic models.
3. **Logical reasoning skills and abilities:** Apply logic to solve quantitative problems, construct rigorous proofs/arguments, and evaluate claims.
4. **Evidence-based decision-making:** Critically evaluate quantitative information using appropriate computational tools to support artificial intelligence and data-driven systems.

---

## Course Contents
1. **Logic, Logical and Critical Reasoning**
   - Introduction and importance of logic
   - Inductive, deductive, and abductive approaches of reasoning
   - Propositions, arguments (valid/invalid), logical connectives, truth tables, and propositional equivalences
   - Logical fallacies and Venn Diagrams
   - Predicates and quantifiers
   - Quantitative reasoning exercises using logical reasoning concepts

2. **Mathematical Modeling and Analyses**
   - Introduction to deterministic models
   - Use of linear functions for modeling in real-world situations
   - Modeling with systems of linear equations and their solutions
   - Elementary introduction to derivatives in mathematical modeling
   - Linear and exponential growth and decay models
   - Quantitative reasoning exercises using mathematical modeling

3. **Statistical Modeling and Analyses**
   - Introduction to probabilistic models
   - Bivariate analysis and scatter plots
   - Simple linear regression model and correlation analysis
   - Basics of estimation and confidence intervals
   - Testing of hypotheses ($z$-test, $t$-test)
   - Statistical inference in decision-making and AI applications
